The task is to score exactly 111 points as many times as possible. When a player busts and can't score 111, the game board reverts to its previous screen, giving the player another chance to score 111. Words can only be used once each board. Players start with 60 seconds on the clock and get an extra 15 seconds for each of the first 5 completed boards. After that, no more bonus time — it's a race against the clock.
